10 Small-Space Storage Ideas to Maximize Every Inch

Living in a small home or apartment doesn't mean you have to live with constant clutter. With a little planning, even the smallest spaces can become organized, practical and comfortable.

Here are 10 simple storage ideas that can help you make better use of every inch of your home.

1. Use Vertical Space

When floor space is limited, look up. Floating shelves, tall bookcases and wall-mounted storage can provide useful space without taking up much floor area.

2. Make Use of Under-Bed Storage

The space underneath your bed is often overlooked. Use low storage boxes or drawers for seasonal clothing, shoes, bedding and other items you don't use every day.

3. Choose Multi-Purpose Furniture

Furniture that serves more than one purpose is especially useful in small homes. Consider a storage ottoman, a bed with built-in drawers or a coffee table with storage.

4. Use the Back of Doors

Doors can provide valuable hidden storage. Over-the-door organizers can be used for accessories, cleaning supplies, shoes or small household items.

5. Add Hooks to Empty Walls

Hooks are inexpensive and easy to install. Use them for bags, hats, towels, kitchen tools or other items that are frequently used.

6. Organize Awkward Corners

Small corners can become useful storage areas with narrow shelves, corner units or small baskets. Don't overlook the spaces between furniture and walls.

7. Use Storage Baskets

Baskets are a simple way to keep everyday clutter under control. Group similar items together and place the baskets on shelves, under tables or inside cabinets.

8. Make Your Kitchen Cabinets Work Harder

Shelf risers, small organizers and hooks can help you use the vertical space inside kitchen cabinets instead of stacking everything in one pile.

9. Declutter Before Buying More Storage

One of the best storage solutions costs nothing. Before purchasing organizers, remove items you no longer use. Less clutter means you need less storage.

10. Give Everything a Home

The easiest organization system to maintain is one where every frequently used item has a designated place. After using something, return it to that location.

Final Thoughts

Small spaces can be organized beautifully when you make use of vertical areas, hidden storage and multi-purpose furniture. Start with one room and one problem area rather than trying to organize your entire home at once.

Small changes can make a surprisingly big difference.
