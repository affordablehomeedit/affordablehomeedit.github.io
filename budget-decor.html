10 Budget Home Décor Ideas

1. Rearrange your furniture

Try a new layout before buying anything.

2. Add greenery

Plants and simple greenery add life and color.

3. Layer textiles

Throws, cushions and rugs can change the mood of a room.

4. Improve lighting

Warm lamps create a softer atmosphere.

5. Declutter first

Removing unnecessary items can make a room feel more polished.

6. Style shelves intentionally

Group books, objects and greenery rather than filling every space.

7. Shop second-hand

Thrifted frames, baskets and small furniture can add character.

8. Refresh one focal area

Concentrate your budget on one wall, corner or seating area.

9. Use what you already own

Move décor between rooms and give existing pieces a new setting.

10. Add personal details

Photos, books and meaningful objects make a home feel finished.
