7 Small Bedroom Organization Ideas

1. Use the walls

Floating shelves, hooks and wall-mounted organizers turn unused vertical space into storage.

2. Divide drawers

Drawer dividers keep smaller items visible and organized.

3. Use the space under the bed

Store seasonal or less frequently used items in low containers.

4. Choose dual-purpose furniture

A storage ottoman, bed with drawers or compact desk can serve more than one purpose.

5. Create a daily reset

Spend five minutes returning clothes, books and accessories to their places.

6. Reduce duplicates

Keeping only what you use makes every storage system more effective.

7. Group similar items

Store similar categories together so you can find things quickly.
